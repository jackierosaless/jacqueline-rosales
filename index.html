Practice / Biography

Jacqueline Rosales

Jacqueline Rosales is an Ecuadorian architect and interdisciplinary researcher based in London. She holds a Master of Architecture from the Architectural Association, where her work explored the intersections of architecture, decolonial theory and ecological knowledge, with a particular focus on the Ecuadorian Amazon.

Her practice moves between spatial investigation, critical writing and experimental design, questioning conventional understandings of territory, cultural memory and the relationship between nature and society.

Through fieldwork, mapping, moving image and architectural propositions, her independent research examines the pressures of urbanisation and extraction on Amazonian territories, and how Indigenous knowledge systems can inform alternative forms of collective spatial organisation.

Alongside her independent research, she works as an architect at Foster + Partners, having previously practised at Ateliers Jean Nouvel and Gensler.
